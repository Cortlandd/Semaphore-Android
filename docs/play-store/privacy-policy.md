# Semaphore Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

Semaphore is a workout timer app for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and what third-party services are involved when you use Semaphore.

## Summary

- Semaphore does not require you to create an account.
- Workout names, durations, progress, and cached cover media are primarily stored on your device.
- Semaphore uses Google AdMob to display banner ads in the free version of the app.
- Semaphore uses Google Play Billing to sell and restore the one-time `remove_ads` purchase.
- If you choose cover media, Semaphore can load content from Klipy and store a local cached copy on your device for faster playback.

## Information Stored by the App

Semaphore stores the following information locally on your device:

- workout names
- workout durations
- workout order
- whether text to speech is enabled for a workout
- completion counts, streaks, and time spent
- timestamps related to workout history
- selected cover media references and cached local media files

This local information is used to power the core workout timer experience, analytics summaries, and playback notifications.

## Advertising

Semaphore uses Google AdMob to display banner ads in the free version of the app.

According to Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosure guidance, AdMob may automatically collect and share certain data such as:

- IP address
- app interactions
- diagnostic information
- device and account identifiers, including advertising-related identifiers when available

This data may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google provides more information in its own documentation and policies:

- https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure
- https://policies.google.com/privacy

If you buy the `remove_ads` product, Semaphore stops showing banner ads in the app.

## Purchases

Semaphore offers a one-time in-app product named `remove_ads`.

Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing, not by Semaphore directly. Semaphore does not collect or store your payment card number or bank information. Semaphore only uses Google Play purchase status to determine whether ads should be shown.

If you reinstall the app or switch devices while using the same Google account, you can restore the ad-removal entitlement through Google Play.

## Cover Media

Semaphore allows you to browse and choose workout cover media through Klipy. If you use that feature, the app may request media from Klipy services and store a cached local copy on your device so the app can display it more quickly later.

Semaphore does not currently provide public user profiles, social sharing, or public uploads.

## Notifications and Background Playback

Semaphore can show notifications while workout timers are running in the background. These notifications can display timer progress, workout names, and cover media associated with the active workout.

## Data Sharing

Semaphore does not sell your personal information.

Semaphore may share data with service providers only as needed to operate app features that rely on them, including:

- Google AdMob for advertising
- Google Play Billing for purchases and purchase restoration
- Klipy for fetching workout cover media

Each provider may process data under its own privacy terms.

## Data Retention and Control

Most Semaphore data is stored locally on your device until you edit it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app.

Because purchases are managed by Google Play, purchase restoration is based on your Google account ownership history rather than local backup state.

## Children

Semaphore is not directed to children under 13.

## Security

Semaphore relies on platform and third-party provider protections for network communication and purchase handling. Third-party SDKs such as Google Mobile Ads and Google Play Billing use encrypted transport for supported network communication.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. When the policy changes, the updated version should be published with a new effective date.
