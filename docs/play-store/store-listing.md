## Full Description

`Semaphore is a simple workout timer built for focused training sessions. Create your routine, add optional cover media, choose how long each workout should run, and play the full sequence from start to finish.`

`Whether you are doing bodyweight intervals, stretching, mobility work, rehab drills, or short daily routines, Semaphore helps you stay locked in without extra clutter.`

`Features:`

- `Create and reorder custom workout routines`
- `Set hours, minutes, and seconds for each workout`
- `Play the full routine from start to finish`
- `Pause, resume, or stop the routine at any time`
- `Keep timers running in the background with notifications`
- `Use optional GIF cover media for visual guidance`
- `Enable optional text to speech for workout names`
- `Track completions, streaks, time spent, and weekly progress`
- `View analytics for your most-used workouts`
- `Remove banner ads with a one-time purchase`

`Semaphore is designed to stay lightweight and easy to understand. Your workout setup and progress are primarily stored on your device, so you can quickly get back to your routine without needing an account.`

`If you want a clean timer app for repeatable routines, Semaphore keeps the experience simple: build your list, hit play, and move.`
